## Town Archetypes (§2A) — sample-set QA, 2026-07-05 (B1.4.14.7)

First-ever render evidence for the four §2A archetypes. 24 samples in
[`QA gallery/Archetypes_2026-07-05/`](QA%20gallery/Archetypes_2026-07-05/):
seeds 11/42/99 × oceanside/mountain/lakeside/desert, full map (768×768) +
LOD-3 anchor crop (lighthouse-or-cannery / sawmill / dock / town-oasis).
Generated by `tools/archetype_qa.mjs`; seeds baked into filenames.

**Verdict: PASS.** Vision-checked (8 in depth, rest spot-checked):
- **Oceanside** — wavy coast, 2-tile beach, pier stub renders into the surf
  (seeds 11/99 clearly), lighthouse white-tower/red-lamp sprite on the shore,
  estuary river mouth reads as drowned channel. No road/rail debris in water.
- **Mountain** — conifer walls rim the map, valley floor open, highway + rail
  river bridges clean, sawmill on the rail (fallback added this session
  guarantees it every seed — probe caught seed 3 generating none).
- **Lakeside** — big lake fused with the river system reads as natural
  reservoir; resort cabins + docks with dirt lanes on the town-facing shore.
- **Desert** — dominant dunes, wobble-edged temperate oasis under the town,
  green riparian ribbons along the river. Strongest of the four.

Minor S3s (logged, not blocking): field/orchard parcels occasionally abut the
beach strip; a pasture fence can land on coastal dunes. Cosmetic, rare.
