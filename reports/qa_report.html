Map Generation Invariants & Urban Plat Standards

Ecological & Agricultural Boundaries

  • Zero Farms in Forests: Farm parcels (Field, Orchard, Pasture) never generate in Biome.Forest or inside standing timber.
  • Zero Crops in Deserts: Deserts maintain natural wind-swept sand dunes; agricultural pivots strictly excluded.
  • Hydrological Setback: 1-tile grass buffer mandatory between all crops/sensitive buildings and lake/river water.
  • Ecotones: 3-tile organic dither transitions between biomes.

Zoning, Parking & Civic Infrastructure

  • Block Purity: 100% of residential homes have street frontage. Block interiors are reserved for backyards, sheds, and pools.
  • Industrial Separation: Factories and warehouses maintain ≥6 tile distance buffer from elementary schools, high schools, and churches.
  • 100% Parking Lot Adjacency: Every commercial and civic anchor is guaranteed an adjacent blacktop parking lot.
  • Multi-Footprint Architecture: Residential zones feature 4 distinct footprints (Ranch w/ garage, Two-story colonial, L-plan, Bungalow w/ veranda).
